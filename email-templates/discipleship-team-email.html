Dear Discipleship Team,

I want to start by saying thank you. Each of you is part of St. Matthew's Discipleship Team — some of you have been assisting in Religious Education and Faith Formation for a while now, and some of you took part in discussions over the last year about rolling out a new program. I'm grateful for your patience and your heart for this ministry.

I also want to apologize for the delay in communicating what's been happening, and for why we didn't meet over the summer. I've been heads-down building a software system to manage registration and record-keeping for our program, and it's now in a usable shape. I've gone ahead and added all of you to the system already. If you think of anyone else who might be interested in joining, please let me know.

What's new: our new program will be rolled out for First Year Holy Communion and First Year Confirmation students. We're using content from fflcm.org — specifically their Pathways suite of programs. We're beginning the year with SEEK, a kerygmatic program that is very simple and meets less frequently than general CCD, though we expect parents to be part of it alongside their children.

This is a massive undertaking, since it includes bringing parents into the process directly, and I'm still in need of Catechists and Faith Formation Leaders to help make it happen. I know each of you has a heart for working with people and building a community of disciples, and I want you to know that you — and your needs — are in my prayers.
